Your turn

Koum, Ka, groove

In his methods, Gabriel has you sing before you play: "Koum" for the bass drum, "Ka" for the snare. Light up some steps, start the loop, set the tempo. Snare hits off beats 2 and 4 sound as ghost notes.

Sound only starts when you start the groove.
