What a clinic covers

Each clinic builds on the Le Rythme Intérieur and From Pad To Groove methods.

  • 01

    Creativity

    Sharpen your sense of improvisation and find new ideas.

  • 02

    Groove

    Improve your groove by working on your sound, all the way to a personal sound.

  • 03

    Practice method

    Organise your sessions and save time while learning.
