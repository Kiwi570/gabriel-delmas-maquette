Count, sing, groove

  1. 01

    Count

    Count and sing the phrase out loud, with the metronome on the off-beat.

  2. 02

    Sing

    Voice, hands and feet answer each other, then swap: that's the switch.

  3. 03

    Groove

    The sticking worked on the pad moves to the kit and becomes a groove: "Koum" on the bass drum, "Ka" on the snare.
