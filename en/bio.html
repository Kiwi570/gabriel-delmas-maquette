Bio & gear

A life in rhythm

From his grandmother's pots and pans to the Moulin Rouge, from Jazz in Marciac to a first album: Gabriel Delmas's story.

First lessons at 4 · trained in Toulouse

See the story

The story

  1. Age 2

    He keeps time on his grandmother's pots and pans.

  2. Age 4

    First drum lessons in Beaumont-de-Lomagne, in the south of France.

  3. Toulouse

    Rock bands, then the Dante Agostini school, where his career begins. Franck Agulhon becomes his teacher.

  4. 2015 · 2016

    Wikidrummer, alongside Tony Royster, Benny Greb and Damien Schmitt.

  5. 2017

    MAKAFUNK, soul and funk, all the way to Jazz in Marciac.

  6. October 2017

    Winner of La Baguetterie's contest, demo at the Moulin Rouge with Sonny Emory, Aaron Spears, Robert Sput and Benny Greb.

  7. October 2019

    The Gabriel Delmas Trio is born: groove compositions, three EPs, jazz festivals.

  8. 2020

    First remote recordings in his home studio, for many artists.

  9. 2021

    Du Pad vers le Groove, his first method, in French and English.

  10. 2022 · 2023

    Coup de cœur of the Confédération musicale de France.

  11. February 2025

    Le Rythme Intérieur, his second book, for every musician.

  12. May 2025

    Opening for Dave Weckl in Toulouse.

  13. May 2026

    New Step, the trio's first album with Gaël Horellou, on Jazz Family.
